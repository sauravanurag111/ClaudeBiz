Business strategy

Dealflow

Tell Claude what you're charging. Dealflow gets you paid. The quote-to-cash back office for freelancers, run by their own Claude, with no AI inside our app.

A freelancer's week

Riya (an illustrative composite) is a freelance brand designer in Bengaluru. On Monday a client says yes to a ₹55,000 logo project on a call. That evening she rebuilds a quote in a Canva template: scope, line items, GST, terms. She sends a PDF on WhatsApp and gets two blue ticks, then silence. She doesn't know if they opened it, agreed or forwarded it to finance. She starts the work anyway, and then spends weeks writing "just checking in" messages she hates sending. She gets paid five weeks later. She already pays for Claude, which could have written every word of that quote and every follow-up, but Claude can't send it, see that it was opened, record a yes, or nudge the client next Tuesday.

Why freelancers first

  • A big, growing market. About 15M freelancers in India, second only to the US1. India's gig workforce is projected to grow from 7.7M (2020–21) to 23.5M by 2029–302. In the US, 64M people (38% of the workforce) freelanced in 20233.
  • Getting paid is the core pain. 61% of Indian freelancers surveyed have gone unpaid at least once, and many wait 2–4 weeks after invoicing4. 71% of US freelancers have had trouble collecting payment, and 81% of those were paid late5.
  • Admin is unpaid time. Freelancers lose about 204 hours a year to admin and paperwork6, and the person doing the chasing is the same person who should be doing billable work.
  • One decision-maker who already uses AI. No procurement process: if it saves a freelancer an evening, they adopt it the same day. Many already pay for Claude.
  • They spread it. Freelancers recommend tools to each other in communities, and every quote reaches a client business.

Primary: freelancers (designers, developers, writers, marketers, consultants) sending 3–20 quotes a month. Secondary: small business owners, such as studios, agencies and consultancies of 2–20 people, with the same workflow at higher volume. Their clients see Dealflow on every quote, which is our path to them.

Why this product, and why not just Claude

1 · Claude writes"Quote Meesho for a logo redesign, ₹40k, half upfront." Scope, items, terms, cover email.
2 · Dealflow sendsEmailed to the client instantly. Freelancer can edit anything; the link updates live.
3 · Client actsOpens, accepts in one click, or asks for changes. Accepting turns it into an invoice.
4 · Until paidFollow-ups on day 3, 7 and 14 stop the moment the client pays.

Claude alone writes well but can't host a quote, track opens, record an acceptance or act after the chat closes. Invoicing software can, but makes you fill in forms. Dealflow joins them: the customer's Claude does the thinking and writing, and Dealflow does everything that has to keep happening afterwards, with no AI bill of its own.

Connect it to Claude (about 1 minute)

  1. In Claude (claude.ai or desktop): Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Dealflow, paste the MCP URL above, leave the OAuth fields empty, click Add.
  3. In a new chat, enable Dealflow in the tools menu and ask for a quote. Allow the tool when Claude asks.

A typical flow

  1. In Claude: "Create a Dealflow quote for Anurag at Meesho (anurag@example.com): logo redesign, 3 concepts, 2 revision rounds, ₹40,000, plus brand guidelines ₹15,000, 18% GST, 50% upfront." Claude writes the scope and terms and calls create_quote.
  2. In Dealflow: Q-0001 is already emailed to the client and on the dashboard, with follow-ups scheduled. The freelancer edits a price and adds a line item; totals update live, and the client's link shows the change immediately.
  3. As the client: open the public link (no login). The dashboard shows "Opened". Click Accept: it becomes invoice INV-0001 with payment details. Click I've paid with a UTR and a payment screenshot. The dashboard shows "Client marked paid".
  4. Back in Claude: "What's unpaid?", then "Mark it paid". Pending reminders are cancelled automatically.

Editing experience

Every field Claude writes can be edited in the browser: client details, scope, line items (add, remove, live totals), discount, tax, currency, validity and due dates, payment terms and T&Cs. Reminders can be edited too (send time, subject, body), sent immediately, or deleted. An activity timeline records who did what: Claude, the freelancer or the client.

What is real vs. simulated in v1

REAL MCP server and tools, Postgres storage, owner editing, public client pages, view tracking, acceptance records, quote-to-invoice conversion, reminder scheduler with automatic cancellation, live deployment.
SIMULATED Payments: no gateway yet. The invoice shows the freelancer's UPI or bank details, the client clicks "I've paid" and the freelancer confirms. Email: real when a Brevo key is configured; otherwise reminders are marked "recorded, email off" in the app. The demo account's payment details are samples.
