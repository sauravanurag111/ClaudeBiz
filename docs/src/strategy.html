Dealflow

Tell Claude what you're charging. Dealflow gets you paid. Quotes that turn into invoices and chase payment, written by the customer's own Claude, with no AI inside our app.

The problem

Freelancers and small agencies (designers, developers, marketers, consultants) lose time and money between "the client said yes on a call" and "the money is in the bank":

  • Writing quotes is slow. Each one means rewriting scope, line items and terms in a Word or Canva template. Invoicing tools only give a blank form.
  • No signal after sending. A PDF on WhatsApp or email gives no sign of whether the client opened it, agreed to it or forwarded it to finance.
  • Chasing payment is awkward, so it doesn't happen. Reminders get sent late, inconsistently, or not at all, and cash flow suffers.

Who it's for

Beachhead: Indian freelancers and 2–20 person studios and agencies who already pay for Claude and send 5–30 quotes a month. They are comfortable with AI, price-sensitive and connected through communities, so word of mouth spreads fast. Next: the same profile in South-East Asia, the US and the UK, priced in USD.

The solution, and why people need it on top of Claude

1 · Claude writes"Quote Acme for a logo redesign, ₹40k, half upfront." Scope, items, terms.
2 · Dealflow storesQuote lands in the dashboard. Owner edits anything.
3 · Client actsOpens a public link and accepts in one click. It becomes an invoice.
4 · Until paidClaude-written reminders go out on schedule and stop when paid.

Claude alone can write a great quote, but it can't host it, see that the client opened it, record an acceptance with a timestamp, keep numbering and status across months, or send an email next Tuesday after the chat has closed. Invoicing software can do those things but makes you fill forms. Dealflow combines both: the writing comes from the AI you already pay for, and Dealflow keeps the deal live until it's paid.

Connect it to Claude (about 1 minute)

  1. In Claude (claude.ai or desktop): Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Dealflow, paste the MCP URL above, leave the OAuth fields empty, click Add.
  3. In a new chat, enable Dealflow in the tools menu and ask for a quote. Allow the tool when Claude asks.

Demo flow (as in the video)

  1. In Claude: "Create a Dealflow quote for Priya at Acme Corp (priya@acme.com): logo redesign, 3 concepts, 2 revision rounds, ₹40,000, plus brand guidelines ₹15,000, 18% GST, 50% upfront." Claude writes the scope and terms and calls create_quote.
  2. In Dealflow: Q-0001 is on the dashboard. The owner edits a price and adds a line item; totals update live. Saving changes the client's page immediately.
  3. As the client: open the public link (no login). The owner's dashboard shows "Opened". Click Accept: it becomes invoice INV-0001 with payment details. Click I've paid with a UTR.
  4. Back in Claude: "Schedule 3 reminders…", then "What's unpaid?", then "Mark it paid". Pending reminders are cancelled automatically.

Editing experience

Every field Claude writes can be edited in the browser: client details, scope, line items (add, remove, live totals), discount, tax, currency, validity and due dates, payment terms and T&Cs. Reminders can be edited too (send time, subject, body), sent immediately, or deleted. An activity timeline records who did what: Claude, the owner or the client.

What is real vs. simulated in v1

REAL MCP server and tools, Postgres storage, owner editing, public client pages, view tracking, acceptance records, quote-to-invoice conversion, reminder scheduler with automatic cancellation, live deployment.
SIMULATED Payments: no gateway yet. The invoice shows the owner's UPI or bank details, the client clicks "I've paid" and the owner confirms. Email: real when a Brevo key is configured; otherwise reminders are marked "recorded, email off" in the app. The demo account's payment details are samples.
